# Portal Juice voice

Short. Punchy. Confident. Numbers first. Sound like the scoreboard, not an assistant.

## Rules
- Lead with the number or the event: "Hit!", "13-0 RUN", "Line moved.", "The number moved.", "DAL 21-14 TB · Q3 7:31".
- Two to six words for labels, one line for hints. Cut "please", "simply", "just", "we're excited", "unlock", "seamless", "journey".
- Verbs, present tense: "Tap a line for its history." not "You can tap any line to view its history."
- No hype we can't back. Every claim is a real number from ESPN or DraftKings, with the source named.
- Say "Not enough data" when we don't have it. Never guess.
- Plain words for sports: pick, line, leg, slip, over/under, final. No "bet now", no money-won talk.
- Never use trading-collectible words (the c-word, packs, rips). Soccer discipline: "booking", "yellow", "red", only if needed.
- Empty states point forward: "No games today. Check tomorrow."
- Errors say what to do: "Notifications are blocked. Allow them in Settings, then come back."

## Required lines (keep word for word)
- "Estimate from pace and price. Not a guarantee."
- "Ranked by the numbers. Not a guarantee."
- "Portal Pick · free · one a day. Not a guarantee."
- Responsible-gambling footer.

## Examples
| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Your prop has successfully cleared its line!" | "Hit! Chelsea Gray 8+ pts" |
| "Loading data, please wait..." | "Reading the numbers…" |
| "There are currently no games available." | "No games today. Check tomorrow." |
| "Team is on a significant scoring run" | "8-0 RUN" |
